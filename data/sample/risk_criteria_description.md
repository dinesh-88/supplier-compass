# Generic Supplier Risk Criteria Descriptions

This document provides detailed descriptions of the generic risk criteria and operational metrics used in the final anonymized supplier risk dataset.

## 1. Risk Indices & Scores

- **Total_Risk_Index:** A weighted average of all risk categories, representing the overall risk profile of the supplier.
- **Industry_Benchmark_Score:** Comparison of the supplier's risk performance against industry peers and regional standards.
- **Media_Sentiment_Score:** Real-time risk monitoring based on news, social media, and public reports.
- **External_Intelligence_Score:** Risk data derived from third-party intelligence and external databases.
- **Sub_Tier_Visibility_Score:** Assessment of risks within the supplier's own supply chain (Tier-N).
- **Labor_Stability_Score:** Risk of strikes, protests, or significant labor disputes at supplier locations.
- **Financial_Health_Score:** Evaluation of the supplier's financial stability, liquidity, and bankruptcy risk.
- **Compliance_Risk_Score:** Risk involving lawsuits, regulatory fines, or legal disputes.
- **Safety_Incident_Score:** Frequency and severity of industrial accidents or safety violations.
- **Environmental_Exposure_Score:** Exposure to geographical risks like earthquakes, floods, or extreme weather events.
- **Digital_Security_Score:** Vulnerability to data breaches, hacking, or digital infrastructure failures.
- **Operational_Resilience_Score:** Risks affecting the day-to-day production and logistics capabilities.
- **Market_Sentiment_Score:** Public and consumer perception risks affecting the supplier.
- **Quality_Risk_Score:** Risk related to product defects, recalls, or quality management failures.
- **Governance_Risk_Score:** Assessment of corporate leadership, ethics, and internal controls.
- **Geopolitical_Risk_Score:** Exposure to humanitarian crises or regional instability.
- **Political_Stability_Score:** Risk of political unrest or government changes affecting operations.
- **Credit_Rating_Score:** Financial creditworthiness based on international rating standards.
- **ESG_Performance_Score:** A comprehensive assessment of the supplier's sustainability and ethical practices, divided into three pillars:
  - Environmental (E): Performance in carbon emissions, waste management, and energy efficiency.
  - Social (S): Management of labor standards, human rights, and health and safety.
  - Governance (G): Corporate ethics, transparency, and anti-corruption measures.

## 2. Operational & Supply Chain Metrics

- **Purchase Order Volume (EUR):** The total financial value of orders placed with the supplier, indicating financial exposure.
- **Order Change Rate:** The frequency of changes to existing orders, expressed as a percentage (0-100%).
- **Supplier Delivery Rating:** A metric measuring the supplier's performance in terms of delivery timeliness and accuracy, expressed as a percentage (0-100%).
- **Order Confirmation Rate:** The rate at which the supplier confirms orders within the requested timeframe, expressed as a percentage (0-100%).
- **Sourcing Risk Level:** Internal assessment of the difficulty in replacing the supplier or finding alternative sources.
- **Supply Chain Complexity:** The complexity of the supply chain associated with the supplier (e.g., logistics routes, sub-tiers).

**Note:** All scores: 0 = no risk, 100 = maximum risk.
